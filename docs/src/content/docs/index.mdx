austeris keeps accounts and entries in several currencies, what you own and what
you owe, loans and deposits, a portfolio of crypto and securities - and computes
net worth, cash flow and a forecast on top of them.

It is built to be run by one person on their own hardware. There is no hosted
version, no account to create with anyone, and no telemetry.
